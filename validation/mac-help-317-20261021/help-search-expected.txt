Usage: search c/student [n/NAME] [l/LEVEL] [p/PHONE] [pp/PARENT_PHONE] [e/EMAIL] [a/ADDRESS]
       [d/DAY] [st/HHMM] [et/HHMM] [s/SUBJECT] [tu/TUTOR_NAME] [rm/ROOM]
       search c/parent [n/NAME] [p/PHONE] [e/EMAIL] [a/ADDRESS]
       [sn/STUDENT_NAME] [d/DAY] [st/HHMM] [et/HHMM] [s/SUBJECT] [tu/TUTOR_NAME] [rm/ROOM]
       search c/tutor [n/NAME] [p/PHONE] [e/EMAIL] [a/ADDRESS]
       [sn/STUDENT_NAME] [d/DAY] [st/HHMM] [et/HHMM] [s/SUBJECT] [rm/ROOM]
       search c/lesson [sn/STUDENT_NAME] [d/DAY] [st/HHMM] [et/HHMM] [s/SUBJECT] [tu/TUTOR_NAME] [rm/ROOM]
Category values ignore case. Supply each lowercase prefix at most once, with a nonblank value.
All filters must match the same person. Name, email and address match text fragments ignoring case;
name/address spaces are normalized. Level and phones match exactly; phone leading zeros matter.
Category-only searches include unlinked people. Missing optional contacts do not match filters.
People results replace the people view and renumber current positions without saving.
Lesson results use a separate panel and preserve people filters and positions.
All lesson filters must match one lesson; sn/ matches its current roster. Text ignores case;
tu/ matches tutor names. Day and HHMM times match exactly. When both times are given, end > start.
Empty lessons are included unless sn/ is supplied. Zero matches display an empty lesson panel.
Student lesson filters match one current enrolment and show its context below the student card.
Parent links use exact phone equality; sn/ and every lesson condition must match the same child.
Tutor conditions must match one assigned lesson and its current roster. Use n/ for tutor names.
Use list or lessons to restore each view.
Examples: search c/student n/Alex l/P1 pp/91234567, search c/lesson tu/mei d/Mon s/Math
